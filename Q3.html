Tim Berner Lee's Original Research vs. Today

The Web's Original Purpose

Tim Berners Lee originally created the internet while working at an organization where physicists studied particles. He designed it to help the researchers find and share information on computers with each other. It was an exciting new way to connect documents and data.

Current Uses of The Web

Today, while researchers still use the web to collaborate on research and share and view documents, the uses have expanded. It is used by the general public for many things, such as communicating, studying, shopping, working, consuming content, and getting news. It has been integrated into almost all aspects of everyday life! It is hard to navigate life without it.

Comparison

Both the original web and the web of today share a similar general purpose, to allow users to access and share information, what's changed since the early days of the web is it's reach. Tim Berners Lee originally designed it for some researchers to access and share information amongst themselves, while today, it's in the hands of billions of people and serves a multitude of purposes.
